Rituals
=======

Blitz costs are Blood, Bone, Crop, and Scroll. Zero-cost offerings are omitted.

Aphrodite Major
  Blitz cost: Blood 7
  Effect: Target Follower switches sides

Aphrodite Minor
  Blitz cost: Blood 2
  Effect: Target Follower runs away with its closest enemy

Apollo Minor
  Blitz cost: Crop 2, Scroll 2
  Effect: Next Turn: Gain 2 Gold, Draw 2 Cards

Apollo Major
  Blitz cost: Crop 3, Scroll 3
  Effect: When you play a Spell, your next Follower costs 1 less (stacks)

Ares Minor
  Blitz cost: Blood 2, Bone 2
  Effect: Your Followers have Sprint

Ares Major
  Blitz cost: Blood 3, Bone 3
  Effect: When one of your Followers kills an enemy, it gains +1/+1 and can attack again

Athena Major
  Blitz cost: Blood 3, Scroll 3
  Effect: When one of your Followers draws blood, get a free spell with cost equal to the damage dealt

Demeter Major
  Blitz cost: Bone 4, Crop 4
  Effect: When a Follower enters Target Player's BattleRow, gain 1 life and draw a card

Demeter Minor
  Blitz cost: Bone 4, Crop 4
  Effect: When one of Target Player's Followers dies, one of your Followers gains its attack

Dionysus Minor
  Blitz cost: Blood 4, Crop 3
  Effect: Sacrifice a Follower: Gain 1 Gold, Draw 2 Cards, Gain 3 Life

Hades Minor
  Blitz cost: Bone 3
  Effect: Summon a copy of the last Follower that died

Hades Major
  Blitz cost: Bone 5
  Effect: At the end of each of target Player's turns, they summon a copy of the last Follower that died

Hephaestus Major
  Blitz cost: Bone 3, Scroll 3
  Effect: When one of target Player's Followers dies, get a copy of every spell that Player cast on on it. The copies costs 1 less.

Hermes Major
  Blitz cost: Scroll 5
  Effect: Whenever a follower attacks the turn it entered, gain a Herald spell

Hermes Minor
  Blitz cost: Scroll 2
  Effect: Steal a card from your opponent's deck. It costs 1 less.

Muse Minor
  Blitz cost: Scroll 2
  Effect: Draw a card

Hestia Minor
  Blitz cost: Crop 2
  Effect: Give a Follower +1/+1

Hera Major
  Blitz cost: Blood 4, Crop 3, Scroll 1
  Effect: When your life total changes, deal 2 damage to a random enemy

Old Ones
  Blitz cost: Blood 3, Bone 3, Crop 3, Scroll 3
  Effect: Summon Typhon and Echidna

Poseidon Major
  Blitz cost: Blood 2, Bone 3, Scroll 3
  Effect: Whenever you sacrifice a follower, gain a free Drown spell

Poseidon Minor
  Blitz cost: Blood 1, Bone 1, Scroll 1
  Effect: Destroy all Followers with the least attack

Zeus Minor
  Blitz cost: Blood 1, Bone 1, Crop 1, Scroll 1
  Effect: Summon a 1/1 copy of a Follower

Zeus Major
  Blitz cost: none (Blood 0, Bone 0, Crop 0, Scroll 0)
  Effect: Deal 4 damage to a target
